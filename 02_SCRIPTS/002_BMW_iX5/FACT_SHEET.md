# BMW iX5 60 xDrive — Verified Fact Sheet

Checked against current official BMW sources on 2026-10-24.

## Germany product page
BMW.de technical data:
- Battery energy content: 141.2 kWh
- Maximum DC charging power: 460 kW
- Range added after 10 minutes HPC: 298–363 km
- Minimum DC charging time 10–80%: 0:23
- Curb weight / Leergewicht: 2,900 kg

Use these as the default **German-market product figures**.

## BMW PressClub Deutschland — High-Power Charging demo
Official BMW Germany press release dated 2026-10-20:
- Gen6 uses 800 V technology
- up to 460 kW maximum / peak DC charging power
- up to 363 km added in 10 minutes (WLTP)
- 141 kWh battery pack net
- 10–80% stated as 22 minutes in the specific high-power-charging demonstration release

## Important discrepancy
BMW's current German consumer technical-data page says **23 minutes minimum** for 10–80%.
The separate German PressClub media-demo release says **22 minutes**.

Operational rule:
- For a general German-market product video: use **23 minutes minimum** from BMW.de.
- If specifically describing the October 20 high-power-charging media demonstration: 22 minutes may be attributed directly to that BMW press release.
- Do not present 22 and 23 as if they are the same data point.

## 800 V
Verified from BMW PressClub Germany / Global Gen6 material.
Do not falsely say it appears in the BMW.de technical-data table if it does not.

## 141 kWh
If using BMW.de alone, say:
- "141.2 kWh battery energy content"

If using PressClub Germany, it supports:
- "141 kWh net"

Do not silently change 141.2 to "net" unless the cited source supports that wording.

## 460 kW
Safe wording:
- "bis zu 460 kW maximale DC-Ladeleistung"
- "bis zu 460 kW Peak-DC-Ladeleistung" when citing the PressClub release

Do not claim a measured sustained charging curve unless we have actual iX5 curve data.

## Charging-taper caution
We do not currently have an official BMW iX5 charging curve proving exactly how long the car holds 460 kW.

Therefore avoid:
- "nur kurz" as a measured iX5 fact
- invented taper timing
- invented average charging power

A generic battery explanation may be used only if clearly framed as general EV charging physics, not as measured iX5 behavior.
