# Next Aura — Brand Identity

## Name
**Next Aura**

Meaning / positioning:
- international rather than specifically German
- futuristic / next-generation feel
- German identity comes from language, topics, typography and execution

## Core positioning
**Automotive Tech + Mobility Engineering + Luxury Machines**

## Audience feel
A German-speaking viewer should recognize the account as:
- technical
- premium
- modern
- curious
- engineering-led

## Brand tone
- premium
- intelligent
- mysterious
- cinematic
- technical
- not hacker / crime / gaming

## Content promise
Explain the engineering detail behind the headline.

## Profile picture
Preferred:
**close-up of the masked presenter**
because it is more recognizable than a generic logo at small TikTok size.

## Username preference
Preferred clean German-facing options:
1. `@nextaura.de`
2. `@nextaura.technik`
3. `@nextaura.techde`
4. `@nextaura.technikde`

Avoid unnecessary numbers, dots and underscores when possible.

## Bio direction
German, concise, curiosity-led, technology-focused.

Approved direction:
**Tech-News, die nicht jeder kennt. 🚗 KI ✈️**
