# Presenter Identity

## Master look
- black / graphite technical hooded jacket
- black ribbed balaclava
- thin rectangular dark / gunmetal glasses
- lightly smoked lenses, eyes still visible
- black broadcast microphone
- dark graphite studio
- subtle blue rim light
- no logos

## Performance
A calm, confident social-media technology creator.
Natural speaking body language, realistic blinking and breathing, controlled gestures.

## Avoid
- mannequin movement
- repeated gestures
- exaggerated influencer acting
- hacker/crime vibe
- finger deformation
- constant on-screen presence

## Usage
Presenter presence is flexible.
Use only when it improves retention or explanation.
