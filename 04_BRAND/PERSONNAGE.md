# Presenter Identity — Canonical

## Master look
- black / graphite technical hooded jacket
- black ribbed balaclava
- thin rectangular gunmetal / dark glasses
- lightly smoked lenses; eyes still visible
- black broadcast microphone
- dark graphite studio
- subtle blue rim / accent light
- no logos

## Character goal
Anonymous, premium, technically credible social-media creator.

Not:
- hacker
- crime aesthetic
- gaming creator
- exaggerated influencer

## Performance
- direct eye contact
- natural speaking body language
- realistic blinking and breathing
- 1–2 purposeful gestures per appearance
- controlled hand movement
- realistic fingers
- stable body proportions
- no sudden deformation

## Usage
Presenter timing is flexible.

Use only when it improves:
- hook
- pattern interrupt
- clarification
- reveal
- payoff

Some videos may use 2 appearances, some 3, some 4, some none.

## Framing
Useful variants:
- centered
- slightly left
- slightly right

This leaves space for graphics without changing identity.

## Flow animation rules
- preserve exact wardrobe and face-covering identity
- minimal camera motion
- stable microphone
- no repeated mannequin gestures
- no dramatic hand waving
- no body warping
- no finger distortion
- silent performance is acceptable when final audio comes from ElevenLabs
