# Final Export & Publish Checklist

## Export
- 9:16 vertical
- 1080×1920 preferred
- stable frame rate matching project/source (commonly 30 fps)
- H.264 MP4
- avoid unnecessary multi-generation re-encoding
- VO clear and undistorted
- no frozen / glitched frames
- no heavy baked blur / tracking artifacts

## Visual QC
- important subject visible on mobile
- TikTok UI does not cover key text
- captions readable
- no other creator watermark
- AI illustrations not presented as real proof

## Story QC
- strong first frame
- strong first 3 sec
- no dead first 8 sec
- no repeated explanation
- ending has payoff

## Monetization QC
If targeting Creator Rewards:
- final duration safely over one minute
- content is original/high-quality
- rights cleared
- no sponsorship conflict
- AI disclosure handled

## Publish package
Save:
- final export filename
- caption
- 5 or fewer relevant hashtags unless a specific reason requires more
- cover text
- AI label decision
- posting time
