# TikTok Account Signals / Warming

## Important
“Warming” is not a guaranteed boost.
The goal is to make the account’s viewing and research behavior consistent with the intended niche.

## Search behavior
Use German searches around:
- BMW Technik deutsch
- Mercedes Technik erklärt
- Porsche Technik deutsch
- Elektroauto 800 Volt
- Schnellladen 800 Volt
- F1 Technik deutsch
- Continental Reifen Technik
- Bosch Automotive Technik
- ZF Fahrwerk Technik
- Megayacht Technik deutsch
- Schiffstechnik deutsch

## Interaction
- watch relevant videos fully when genuinely useful
- like selectively
- save strong hook / editing references
- follow a small number of relevant creators
- use “Not interested” on unrelated repeated content

## Content identity
During the first ~20 posts, keep the account heavily centered on Automotive / Auto-Tech.
Adjacent mobility is allowed, but should not dominate.
