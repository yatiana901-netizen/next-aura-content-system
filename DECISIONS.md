# Canonical Decision Log

Only durable decisions belong here.

## D001 — Repository policy
The repository stores approved / canonical work only.
Drafts and failed experiments stay outside main canonical files.

## D002 — Niche
Next Aura = Automotive Tech + Mobility Engineering + Luxury Machines.

## D003 — Visual-first scripting
If a sentence requires nearly impossible footage, rewrite the sentence instead of forcing the visual.

## D004 — Presenter
No fixed timing or number of appearances.
Use only when it improves retention or explanation.

## D005 — Editing
Hard cuts are the default.
Transitions and SFX are selective, not automatic.

## D006 — Heavy blur / tracking
Do not bake heavy moving blur or auto-tracking into full videos.
Testing caused lag, glitches and quality loss.

## D007 — EditPilot
Use primarily for structure / simple transitions.
Do not rely on it for selecting custom imported SFX.

## D008 — Monetization-first sourcing
Rights and originality are production gates.
Do not assume online / YouTube / press footage is automatically monetization-safe.

## D009 — AI
Realistic AI-generated / significantly AI-edited media must be handled transparently and labeled when required.

## D010 — Evidence vs hypothesis
Separate:
- platform-supported principles,
- official technical facts,
- our creative hypotheses.

Duration ranges, micro-hook timing and fixed scene timing are experiments until account data supports them.

## D011 — CTA
Use topic-native interaction when it improves the story.
Generic “follow for more” is not the default CTA.

## D012 — Technical numbers
Verify exact figures against the primary source and avoid mixing market-specific specifications.
