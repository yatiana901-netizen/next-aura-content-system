# Editing Rules

## Rhythm
Editing follows meaning, not a timer.
A new shot should normally represent a new idea or a useful visual reset.

## Transitions
Mostly hard cuts.
Use simple push / zoom / short blur only on major conceptual changes.
Avoid gaming transitions, spins, heavy glitches, aggressive flashes and unnecessary effects.

## Sound design
No requirement to add SFX to every cut.
Use only a few recognizable effects on major moments:
hook, reveal, proof, major chapter change, final presenter entrance.
Voice-over always dominates.

## CapCut
Prefer light manual keyframes over automated heavy tracking.
For horizontal clips:
- crop to 9:16 when it preserves the subject;
- otherwise use a light/static background treatment;
- avoid heavy motion blur or frame-by-frame tracking that introduces lag or glitches.

## Quality control
Check:
- first 3 seconds
- dead time
- duplicated ideas
- visual/VO mismatch
- overlong presenter shots
- unreadable crops
- unnecessary effects
