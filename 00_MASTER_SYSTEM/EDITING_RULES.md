# Editing Rules — Canonical

## Rhythm
Editing follows meaning, not a fixed timer.

A new shot should normally:
- introduce a new idea,
- clarify the current idea,
- or create a useful pattern reset.

Do not change shots just to look “fast”.

## First 8 seconds
The opening should be the strongest available visual sequence.
Avoid dead space, setup, logos or long presenter intros.

## Transitions
Default = **hard cuts**.

Use simple transitions only at major conceptual changes:
- short push
- short zoom
- short blur
- subtle whip if justified

Avoid:
- spins
- heavy glitches
- gaming transitions
- aggressive flashes
- 3D template effects
- transition on every cut

## Sound design
Do not add SFX to every cut.

Use only a few familiar effects on major moments:
- hook
- hardware / mechanism reveal
- chapter change
- proof / number
- presenter entrance

Voice-over always dominates.

## CapCut / EditPilot lesson
EditPilot is useful for:
- rough structural edits
- transitions
- trimming / first-pass work

Do **not** rely on EditPilot to choose imported SFX correctly when it keeps substituting CapCut-library sounds.

For custom SFX:
- place them manually when needed
- keep the count low
- trim and level them manually

## Horizontal clips
Prefer:
- intelligent crop
- light manual pan / zoom
- static or light background fill only if needed

Avoid:
- heavy auto tracking
- frame-by-frame motion blur
- baked effects that create lag or glitches

## Final QC
Check:
- first 3 seconds
- first 8 seconds
- dead time
- visual/VO mismatch
- repeated idea
- overlong presenter sections
- unreadable crop
- unnecessary transition
- unnecessary SFX
- ending payoff
