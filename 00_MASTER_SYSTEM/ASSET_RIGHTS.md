# Asset Rights & Footage Safety — Canonical

## Core rule
**A clip being online, on YouTube, or in a press room does not automatically mean it is licensed for monetized TikTok reuse.**

## Source priority for monetization-first videos
1. Footage we created ourselves
2. Footage with explicit reuse / commercial / social rights
3. Official press assets whose terms clearly permit the intended use
4. Properly licensed stock footage
5. Third-party clips only when we have permission or a defensible legal basis

## Never assume
- "official footage" = automatically monetization-safe
- "editorial use" = commercial social reuse
- "short clip" = automatically fair use
- "everyone reposts it" = permission

## Per-asset rights record
For every important external asset, record:
- exact filename
- source URL
- owner / publisher
- date downloaded
- license / permission status
- permitted use
- attribution requirement
- proof location (screenshot / PDF / email / license)
- whether watermark is present
- whether it is safe for Creator Rewards workflow

## Watermarks
Avoid clips carrying another creator's watermark, especially for Creator Rewards.

## Audio / SFX
Imported SFX also need a known license.
Do not build the final sound design from random downloaded files with unknown rights.

## Copyright risk
TikTok can remove infringing content and issue copyright strikes.
Treat rights clearance as a production gate, not an afterthought.

## Asset decision
If rights are unclear:
- replace the clip,
- create our own diagram / AI illustration,
- use licensed stock,
- or leave the claim out.
