## How to use / طريقة الخدمة
1. User: sends a number, e.g. `1` / `6`, or `Step 4 Mercedes Holo Display`.
2. Assistant: read that stage's file, prior outputs, and master SOP. Use project context already present; do not repeat answered questions.
3. Execute the **named stage only**, fully where feasible. Deliver its real artifact(s), cited facts if researched, QA findings and blockers.
4. **Before sending results**, self-review content and details. Mark `PASS`, `PARTIAL`, or `BLOCKED`; never claim a file, actual audio review, render, or upload occurred without evidence.
5. If inputs/tools missing: give the strongest verified partial output, name exactly what's missing and how the next stage depends on it; do not invent timestamps, media, rights or analytics.
6. User confirms/advances by sending the next number. Do not auto-publish or make a major unapproved creative pivot.

## Permanent rules

- **Germany-first:** German scripts, titles, descriptions, technical keywords; Moroccan Darija for user coordination.
- **No music of ANY kind:** not in SFX, original footage, risers or endings. Only actually vetted nonmusical sound effects; source sounds only when verified clean and appropriate.
- **9:16** preferred, typically 1080x1920/30fps; frame-accurate word/card/SFX alignment; intelligent camera follow.
- **No forced 60-second padding, no constant subtitles by default**, no distracting overlays or cheap effects.
- **No false certainty:** technical decode is not substitute for watching/listening; original creator's rights are not conveyed by availability; no fabricated video files/links.
- **German publishing:** exactly five relevant hashtags per video; TikTok/YouTube packages separate; no unauthorized publishing.
- **Two versions:** compare narratives and shot pool; do not mass-upload near-identical variants with the same VO.
- **Assets:** raw videos/audio, licensed media and private information remain on private Drive/local, not in GitHub.
