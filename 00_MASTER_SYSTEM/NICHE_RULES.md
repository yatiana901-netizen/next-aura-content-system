# Niche Rules — Canonical

## Core identity
**Next Aura = Automotive Tech + Mobility Engineering + Luxury Machines**

The account is not:
- generic car reviews,
- general consumer tech,
- random trending news.

The account explains machines, mobility and engineering.

## Recommended content mix
- **60–70% Automotive / Auto-Tech**
- **~15% F1 / Motorsport Engineering**
- **~10% Ships / Yachts / Marine Engineering**
- **5–10% Aviation / Trains / Drones / eVTOL / Trucks**
- **Up to 5% controlled experiments**

## Core automotive topics
- EVs, batteries, fast charging, motors, inverters
- engines, transmissions, cooling
- brakes, tires, suspension, steering
- aerodynamics
- ADAS, sensors, vehicle AI
- cockpit displays and vehicle interfaces
- manufacturing
- patents
- recalls with a technical angle
- prototypes and concept technology
- suppliers: Bosch, Continental, ZF, Brembo, Mahle, Schaeffler, etc.

## Motorsport
Strong fit when the angle is technical:
- F1 aerodynamics
- brake cooling
- hybrid systems
- tires
- suspension
- materials
- gearbox
- thermal management

Avoid gossip, driver drama and generic race recap.

## Ships and yachts
Strong fit when framed as **luxury machine explained** or marine engineering:
- propulsion
- azipods
- stabilizers
- hybrid / LNG / electric systems
- docking systems
- bridge automation
- underwater garages
- retractable pools
- helipads
- desalination
- power management
- glass / structural engineering

Avoid celebrity-yacht gossip unless the engineering is the real story.

## Aviation / rail / drones
Allowed when there is a strong engineering angle:
- jet engines
- electric / hydrogen aviation
- wing technology
- landing gear
- high-speed rail
- maglev
- regenerative braking
- eVTOL
- autonomous drones

## Topic gate
If the story involves:
**mobility, propulsion, energy storage, charging, vehicle electronics, aerodynamics, materials, control systems, performance or transport engineering**, it is probably inside the niche.

## Audience consistency rule
A viewer who enjoys one Next Aura video should reasonably enjoy the next one.
Do not jump from BMW engineering to unrelated phones, gaming or random AI tools.
