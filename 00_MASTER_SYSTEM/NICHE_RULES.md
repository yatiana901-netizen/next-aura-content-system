# Niche Rules

## Core identity
**Next Aura = Automotive Tech + Mobility Engineering + Luxury Machines**

## Recommended mix
- 60–70% Automotive / Auto-Tech
- 15% F1 / Motorsport Engineering
- 10% Ships / Yachts / Marine Engineering
- 5–10% Aviation / Trains / Drones / eVTOL / Trucks
- Up to 5% controlled experiments

## Strong-fit topics
- Cars, EVs, batteries, charging, engines, motors, inverters
- Brakes, tires, suspension, steering, aerodynamics, cooling
- ADAS, sensors, vehicle AI, displays and cockpit technology
- Manufacturing, patents, recalls, suppliers and prototypes
- F1 engineering
- Trucks and megawatt charging
- High-speed rail / maglev / regenerative braking
- Aircraft engines, wings, landing gear, electric or hydrogen aviation
- eVTOL and drones
- Cargo ships, cruise ships, propulsion, azipods
- Superyachts: stabilizers, hybrid propulsion, power systems, underwater garages, retractable systems

## Weak-fit / avoid
- Generic consumer tech
- Random AI news unrelated to mobility
- Celebrity yacht gossip without engineering
- Travel content
- General sports gossip
- Random lifestyle trends

## Gate
If a topic involves **mobility, propulsion, energy storage, vehicle electronics, aerodynamics, materials or transport engineering**, it is probably eligible.
