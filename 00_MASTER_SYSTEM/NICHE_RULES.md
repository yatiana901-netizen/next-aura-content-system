# Niche Rules — Canonical

## Core identity
**Next Aura = Extreme Machines + Engineering & Technology** — German-language stories about surprising real machines and how they work. **User-approved expanded scope, 2026-11-07:** cars/motorcycles, planes, ships, civilian and military vehicles, military technology, submarines, heavy industry, rail, rockets/space, robots and drones. Brand coherence comes from *engineering/technical explanation*, not a single vehicle category.

The account is not:
- generic car reviews,
- general consumer tech,
- random trending news.

The account explains machines, mobility and engineering.

## Category mix — flexible discovery (updated 2026-11-07)
**Do NOT restrict trend discovery to cars, or enforce the previous 60–70% automotive percentage as a hard quota.** For each research run, scan all relevant categories; then select the strongest *verified Germany-relevant rising topic* by actual evidence, visual explainability, originality and footage feasibility. Maintain channel consistency via the shared "how this machine works" engineering format; after several tests adjust category proportions using account retention and follow data.

Search domains: cars / motorcycles / motorsport / trucks; aviation (civil and military); ships / marine / aircraft carriers / submarines; defense equipment and military technology; space / rockets; trains / rail; drones; industrial machines / robotics / giant equipment. An event needs a genuine machine/engineering angle; avoid generic geopolitics, war propaganda, and tactical instructions for harmful use.

- Aircraft technology, fighter aircraft systems in factual high-level terms, engine/aerodynamics/avionics, helicopters, spacecraft, rockets, launch systems and reusability
- Naval/large ships, aircraft carriers, submarine design, marine propulsion, shipyards, giant floating machines
- Tanks, armored vehicles, sensing/radar and protective systems, defense-industry engineering, manufacturing, materials, mobility and history at an explanatory level
- Huge mining, construction and industrial machines, robots, turbines and machines with a surprising measurable capability
- **Safety/editorial:** factual civilian-facing overview and historical/comparative engineering; no weapon build instructions, attack optimization or operational assistance, no sensational false claims

## Topic gate
If the story involves:
**machines, mobility, propulsion, power generation, energy storage, charging, aerodynamics, materials, control/sensing systems, aerospace, marine engineering, military technology at an explanatory level, industrial design or transport engineering**, it is probably inside the niche.

## Audience consistency rule
A viewer who enjoys one Next Aura video should reasonably enjoy the next one.
Do not jump from BMW engineering to unrelated phones, gaming or random AI tools.
