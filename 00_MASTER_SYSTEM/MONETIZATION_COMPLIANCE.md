# Monetization & Creator Rewards — Canonical

## Goal
Build videos that can grow the account **and** preserve eligibility for TikTok Creator Rewards when the account becomes eligible.

## Account-level requirements to remember
TikTok currently requires, among other conditions:
- eligible country/region
- account in good standing
- **Personal Account** (not Business)
- authentic account information
- age requirement
- at least 10,000 followers
- at least 100,000 video views in the previous 30 days

Always verify the current official TikTok requirements before relying on them.

## Video-level Creator Rewards gate
For a monetization-targeted video:
- high-quality
- original
- safely **over one minute** in the final export when the story supports it
- uploaded after joining the program
- compliant with Community Guidelines, Terms and copyright rules

Do not pad weak content merely to exceed one minute.

## Originality risk
TikTok may consider the following non-original:
- copied videos
- other creators' watermarked footage
- other people's videos with only light modifications
- compilations of third-party clips/images without meaningful new personal ideas
- loops / photo-only / text-only low-effort formats

## Next Aura originality strategy
A monetization-first video should maximize:
- original German narration
- original research and technical explanation
- original masked-presenter footage
- custom diagrams / technical animations
- original AI illustrations where appropriate and disclosed
- rights-cleared exact-project footage only where necessary

Do not assume that heavy editing alone automatically makes third-party footage eligible as original content.

## Sponsored-content rule
Sponsored content is treated differently and may not qualify for Creator Rewards.
Keep future sponsorship workflow separate from Rewards-focused editorial content.

## Qualified-view reminder
Rewards use qualified views, not raw views.
Do not optimize for fake / paid / artificial engagement.
