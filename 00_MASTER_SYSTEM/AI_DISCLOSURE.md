# AI Disclosure — Canonical

## Why this matters
Next Aura uses:
- AI-generated presenter visuals / motion
- AI-generated technical illustrations
- AI-assisted visual transformations
- synthetic voice tools

TikTok requires labeling for realistic AI-generated content in covered cases and encourages disclosure for content generated or significantly edited with AI.

## Safe operating rule
If the final video contains realistic AI-generated or significantly AI-edited imagery / video, use TikTok's AI-generated-content disclosure when required or when there is reasonable ambiguity.

## Important
TikTok states that applying the AI-generated label does not reduce distribution by itself as long as the content follows the rules.

## Never
- present an AI illustration as real test footage
- fabricate a real event
- make a real person appear to say or do something they did not
- use misleading synthetic "proof"

## Visual labeling inside the story
When necessary, use clear language such as:
- "Visualisierung"
- "schematisch"
- "Konzept"
- "AI-Visualisierung"

Do not over-label every harmless diagram if it is already obviously illustrative; prioritize clarity and honesty.
