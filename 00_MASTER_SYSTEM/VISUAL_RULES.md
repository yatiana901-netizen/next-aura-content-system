# Visual Rules — Canonical

## Source priority
1. Official / original footage of the exact project
2. Legitimate licensed or press footage
3. Technical diagrams / screenshots
4. Supporting stock footage
5. AI illustration only when real footage cannot show the mechanism

## Exact-project search
Search exact project names first:
- company
- model / project title
- technical feature
- official demo title
- year
- press-room wording

Use generic keywords only for explanatory filler.

## Shot matching
Every important sentence needs a specific visual.
Do not use random B-roll just to keep the screen moving.

## TikTok format
- 9:16
- keep important information away from UI-safe zones
- crop horizontal footage only when the subject remains understandable
- if a full crop destroys technical information, use a restrained background treatment inside CapCut

## Horizontal-footage lesson
Do **not** bake heavy tracking or heavy moving blur into the whole video.
It caused lag, glitches and quality loss in testing.

Preferred approach:
- sharp foreground
- static/light background treatment only when necessary
- light manual keyframes
- 2–3 keyframes rather than continuous tracking

## Presenter
The presenter is not on a fixed schedule.
Use only when it adds value.

Keep wardrobe identity consistent, but vary:
- crop
- angle
- gesture
- lighting intensity

## AI visuals
Use AI for:
- vibration fields
- airflow
- internal mechanisms
- battery / high-voltage architecture
- pressure / heat / sound localization

Never present AI visualization as real experimental proof.
