# NEXT AURA — TikTok + YouTube Shorts publishing workflow
**Decision agreed 2026-11-03:** Publish each suitable finished vertical video on **both TikTok and YouTube Shorts**. Shorts is the YouTube format for current ~35–55s 9:16 German automotive edits, not conventional horizontal long-form uploads. Long-form YouTube may be explored as a separate production type later.

## Source and rights
- Export **one pristine original MP4**, 9:16 (target 1080×1920), no music and no TikTok watermark.
- Upload that **original** directly to TikTok and YouTube Shorts independently; do not download from one service to upload to the other.
- Technical/copyright/commercial use rules remain platform-specific. Source footage and SFX must have appropriate rights; crop, mirror, narration and re-editing do not automatically clear copyright or guarantee monetization.
- For YouTube, reused-content monetization policies may assess the substantive originality of commentary and transformation in addition to copyright.

## Mandatory deliverables with every Next Aura video
1. **Main MP4** with QA of German spoken word ↔ image ↔ info card ↔ SFX synchronization, no Presenter until user opts back in.
2. **TikTok Publishing Pack:** TikTok vertical miniatura/cover, researched German SEO caption/title, exactly **5 relevant hashtags**, posting date and time **for Germany and converted Morocco**.
3. **YouTube Shorts Publishing Pack:** YouTube-optimized German Shorts title, concise description/hashtags tailored to YouTube search, suitable cover or selected frame with platform-appropriate publishing instructions, and **independently researched posting date/time** for Germany and Morocco.
4. **Source/fact and media-rights notes**, especially future vs current specs and whether footage is licensed.
5. **Post-performance comparison plan:** watch completion, rewatch, follows/subscribers, shares and retention within each platform; recalibrate future packaging and timing based on per-platform analytics.

## Timing rule: NO universal hour
- TikTok and YouTube Shorts can go live at the same time **if evidence supports it**, or at different times. Do NOT arbitrarily force a gap, repeat an old slot, or guarantee performance from a time choice.
- For *each specific upload*, research latest credible audience/activity indicators, weekday, niche and when available account analytics (TikTok Analytics and YouTube Studio audience activity). Give a **concrete best-effort time** for each platform and convert accurately to Morocco time.
- Specific minutes are **allowed** (e.g. 18:07) if justified or clearly identified as experiment. Never invent minute-level precision from hourly averages.
- No fixed hours or dates should be committed as universal defaults in GitHub. Project-specific schedules should be discussed separately and dated when needed.

## Platform packaging differences
### TikTok
- Keep short, hooky, truthful German caption with key technical/product terms and EXACTLY 5 related hashtags.
- TikTok vertical 9:16 miniature: clear subject, very short mobile-readable headline, safe zones.
- Consider the platform's native thumbnail/text placement.

### YouTube Shorts
- Native 9:16 Shorts upload, no TikTok watermark. Customize the German headline for how people search on YouTube.
- Avoid cramming the exact TikTok title/hashtags into the YouTube fields by default.
- Thumbnail workflow must reflect actual YouTube Shorts account/device options; if custom image uploading is restricted, choose the best available video frame and use supported edit controls.
- Use honest metadata (avoid sensationalism that changes the meaning of verified figures).

## Handoff to another ChatGPT
Every completed Next Aura vertical video **must arrive with BOTH distinct publishing packs**, not just a TikTok pack. Research posting time separately for each platform and output a ready-to-copy German title and hashtags for each. The video edit remains one high-quality master unless platform-specific visual or licensing rules require a different render. Never claim a video is posted or a thumbnail uploaded unless the platform confirms it.

This is a living operating standard: keep technical QA, thumbnails, SEO and scheduling decisions separate, and update based on real TikTok and YouTube Shorts performance.
