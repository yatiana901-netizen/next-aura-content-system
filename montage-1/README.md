### Non-negotiable editing standards
- **No music.** German VO is central; select restrained SFX from the provided library. Repeating a useful SFX is allowed; no artificial quota.
- **No masked presenter for now.** Presenter and background removal stay disabled until user explicitly re-enables them.
- **Fast clean narrative.** Hook in first 8 seconds, meaningful shots, no fillers, no stretching to one minute for its own sake.
- **Exact trims.** Every source in/out belongs to a single uninterrupted shot; inspect boundaries frame by frame to prevent accidental mixed scenes.
- **Vertical reframing.** Turn 16:9 into 9:16 using smooth camera-follow/subject tracking, not a careless fixed center crop; avoid black bars.
- **Selective flipping.** CapCut `Mettre en miroir` can help some shots, but never reverse texts, logos, or misrepresent technical details. Flipping does not remove copyright.
- **Motion.** Hard cuts are primary, with occasional subtle movement-matched whip, motion blur, push-in, speed ramp or other realistic transitions where they improve the scene.
- **Info cards.** Small/medium, clearly legible on mobile, minimalist, slightly transparent, color-matched to lighting. Very brief natural easing/slide/scale. Only high-value facts, not permanent subtitles.
- **Synchronization is mandatory.** Determine onset of the **spoken German word** from real VO, then synchronize corresponding shot, card, and SFX transient. Keep overlays within the relevant shot. Listen and watch at 0.5× then 1× on the actual export.
- **Sound design.** Review each sound, cut silent padding, balance gain and fades, keep VO intelligible, reject weak or unrelated effects.
- **Original contribution and rights.** Prefer sources licensed for reuse, add authentic commentary/editing, keep evidence. Cropping, mirroring and effects do **not** establish fair use or eliminate claims.
- **Honest final QA.** Verify sync, exact cuts, hidden transitions, readability, silence, black frames, loudness, exported frame count and file decode. Disclose unverified checks.
