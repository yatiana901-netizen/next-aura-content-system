## Corrections vs preliminary Stage 01 / earlier draft
1. `Stuttgart, 03 Nov` is **the official PRESS RELEASE dateline**. Journalists examined the test demonstrator at the Sindelfingen research centre. Don't say Mercedes demonstrated the prototype in Stuttgart city simply from press dateline.
2. `Holo Display` should not be depicted as a free-floating **3D hologram**. It's light projected onto/optically reflected via special film in windshield. Not the other mini/micro-LED-in-glass technology.
3. The design potentially separates driver/passenger views; no claim that everyone sees different videos in every seat position / conditions. Avoid presenting entertainment as legal driver video-watching during ordinary driving.
4. Claims of sunlight visibility are Mercedes' claim, not independently demonstrated under all conditions.
5. The **20% brightness** and exact test vehicle are journalist-reported contextual demo details; not necessary for baseline VO.
6. Reports of imperfect resolution and distorted picture are relevant but attributed to specific journalists; Mercedes has not announced market-ready quality.
7. No verified launch, pricing, 4K specification or release in a purchasable vehicle. All such future claims must be conditional.
8. US media footage is editorial-only; review actual terms for intended channel and permissions before using; availability is not reuse clearance.
9. Reconcile the earlier draft implying the full windshield is already a screen: current prototype has a limited visible area; future expansion is exploratory.

## Safe factual logic for Stage 04 script
- A Mercedes research **prototype** uses a dashboard projector with special transparent windshield film.
- The film steers/refocuses light toward certain viewpoints so passengers do not see the same display content.
- Mercedes proposes navigation and driver information as well as passenger-side entertainment.
- It is not in production, and prototype image quality has limitations.
- End on a grounded open question, not a claim that commercial vehicles already use it.
