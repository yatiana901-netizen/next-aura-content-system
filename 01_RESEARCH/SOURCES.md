# Priority Sources

## Automotive OEM
- BMW PressClub
- Mercedes-Benz Media
- Porsche Newsroom
- Audi MediaCenter
- Volkswagen Newsroom

## Suppliers
- Bosch
- Continental
- ZF
- Brembo
- Mahle
- Schaeffler

## Motorsport
- FIA
- Formula 1 technical / official sources
- manufacturer motorsport press rooms

## Engineering / science
- official research institutes
- university labs
- primary papers when relevant
- official company technical releases

## Marine / yachts
Prefer:
- shipyards
- marine propulsion companies
- yacht builders
- classification / engineering sources
- official technical demonstrations

## Rule
Use primary / official sources whenever possible.
Secondary reporting is useful for discovery and context, not as a replacement for the original technical source when the latter exists.
