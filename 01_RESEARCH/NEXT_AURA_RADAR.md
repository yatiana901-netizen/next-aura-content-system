# Next Aura Radar — Canonical

## Purpose
Catch strong fresh stories early without flooding the creator.

## Fresh alert channel
**Gmail only** for breaking/fresh opportunities.

### Email threshold
Send an alert only when:
- the story was published or materially updated within the last 24 hours,
- it fits the Next Aura niche,
- it has a strong visual / engineering angle,
- it is worth early-mover attention.

### Exception
An older story may trigger an email only if a **new** technical detail, official demo, test result, patent, regulation, recall, or primary-source update appeared within the last 24 hours.

### Gmail alert format
Keep it short:
1. What is new
2. Why it matters for Next Aura
3. Exact publication/update date
4. Primary source
5. Urgency: POST NOW / TODAY / CAN WAIT

Do not send:
- full scripts
- shot plans
- long analysis
- 24–72h old ideas
- evergreen topics

## Older / less urgent topics
Stories roughly 24–72 hours old, evergreen engineering ideas and deeper backlog topics are handled **manually in ChatGPT only** when requested.

## Duplicate control
Do not resend the same topic/source if it was already alerted.

## Monitoring cadence
Hourly condition watch.

## Niche coverage
- Automotive / EV / batteries / charging
- vehicle engineering
- F1 / motorsport engineering
- suppliers: Bosch, Continental, ZF, Brembo, Mahle, Schaeffler
- trucks
- aviation engineering
- trains
- drones / eVTOL
- mega-ships
- superyachts
