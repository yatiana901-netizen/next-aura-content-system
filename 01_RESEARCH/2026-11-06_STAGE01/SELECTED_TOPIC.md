## Core story & status
Mercedes-Benz unveiled an innovative **holographic windshield display research prototype** in Stuttgart on **3 November 2026**. A dashboard projector and special laminated holographic optical elements selectively reflect the projected light; the view angle can differ between driver and passenger. It is **under research/development, not a series feature or confirmed vehicle option**. No series date is announced.
