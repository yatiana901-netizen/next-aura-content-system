# Image Generation — Engineering Visual Rules

## Goal
Create believable technical illustration frames for mechanisms that real footage cannot show.

## Style
- photorealistic CGI
- premium engineering documentary
- dark graphite / industrial environment when appropriate
- realistic metal, glass, rubber, plastic, composite materials
- restrained blue accents
- realistic lighting

## Technical integrity
- do not invent components unnecessarily
- preserve geometry across start/end frames
- do not add fake sparks, energy beams or sci-fi holograms
- no labels unless specifically requested
- no fake evidence framing

## TikTok
Default vertical 9:16 when the image will become a Flow video.

## Battery / EV visual lesson
Prefer:
- stable battery geometry
- visible high-voltage cabling
- realistic power electronics
- camera move toward the mechanism

Avoid:
- battery opening itself without reason
- floating cells
- exploded parts if not requested
- neon electricity
