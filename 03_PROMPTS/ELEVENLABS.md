# ElevenLabs — Canonical VO Rules

## Voice target
Natural German social-media creator.
Energetic enough for retention, but not a commercial announcer.

## Script formatting
- short sentences
- clean punctuation
- avoid overlong nested clauses
- use line breaks only where a natural pause helps
- avoid abbreviations that may be misread
- write units in a way the selected voice pronounces reliably

## Take selection
Compare takes on:
- hook energy
- pace
- natural pauses
- pronunciation
- consistency
- lack of dead air

Keep one main take when possible so the voice does not change character mid-video.

## Editing
Allowed:
- trim silence at beginning / end
- remove obvious dead air between segments
- preserve natural breaths and cadence

Avoid:
- aggressive time-stretch
- cutting inside words
- over-compressing pauses until speech sounds synthetic

## Duration
Write for the target duration first.
Do not depend on extreme speed changes to make the script fit.
